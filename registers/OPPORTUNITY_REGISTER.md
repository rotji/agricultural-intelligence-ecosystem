## OPP-0001 — Seed-quality and supplier-to-farmer coordination

- **Date discovered:** 19 November 2026
- **Origin:** Agricultural Intelligence Daily Brief, 19 November 2026; NAERLS survey findings and NASC directory leads as reported in that brief.
- **Problem hypothesis:** Seed quality and access to suitable agricultural inputs may constrain crop production. The brief reported seed-quality constraints and a decline in soybean production despite increased cultivated area.
- **Geography:** Nigeria; initial supplier investigation in Plateau State.
- **Product/value chain:** Seeds and planting materials, initially including soybean; scope remains open to other crops and seed products.
- **Potential opportunity types:** Supplier-to-farmer coordination, demand aggregation, referral, authorised distribution, seed availability information and field-performance information.
- **Existing providers/alternatives:** NASC-listed enterprises, licensed seed companies, agro-dealers, agronomists and farmer organisations.
- **Evidence:** Institutional survey findings and directory entries. Current licence status, operational status, stock, varieties, prices and willingness to partner have not yet been verified.
- **Evidence level:** Source-reported institutional findings; supplier directory entries are leads, not proof of current stock or operation.
- **Confidence:** Medium that the underlying issue merits investigation; low/unknown for any specific commercial route.
- **Status:** Discovered.
- **Priority:** A — initial investigation because the issue has a reported production link and identifiable local supplier leads.
- **Objective:** Determine whether there is a real, accessible coordination or partnership role that does not require purchasing inventory.
- **Next action:** Verify the three Plateau-listed seed businesses: Fruits and Veggies Global Ltd (Zawan, Jos); Romarey Ventures Nig. Ltd (Mangu); Savannah Seed Limited (Jos).
- **Dependencies:** Current licence and operational status; varieties; availability; stock; price; quantity; delivery coverage; partnership/referral permissions and terms.
- **Owner:** Unassigned.
- **Capital level:** Level 0 may be possible for information or coordination under an agreed arrangement; not established.
- **Review date:** 21 November 2026.
- **Caution:** No supplier has been contacted or verified through this record. No inventory or income opportunity is confirmed.
