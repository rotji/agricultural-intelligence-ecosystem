# Agricultural Ecosystem Intelligence Daily Brief — 22 November 2026

**Research cutoff:** 22 November 2026, approximately 21:25 Africa/Lagos  
**Geographic scope:** Plateau State (all 17 LGAs), Nigeria (all states and FCT), and relevant international markets  
**Overall assessment:** Nigeria's 2026 harvest outlook is improving, and new federal input/market-stabilisation measures have been announced. However, neither announcement yet establishes accessible supply, Plateau allocations, distribution channels or lower retail prices. Price-platform data for maize and other staples remain inconsistent and mostly estimated. The immediate priority is to verify implementation and improve local price evidence—not to buy stock or build a platform.

## 1. Five-minute front brief

### Five developments that matter

1. **National cereal output is forecast at 29.39 million tonnes, up 1.84%.** NAERLS' 2026 wet-season survey says maize and rice are expected to drive the gain. Flooding, pests and low extension coverage remain material risks. BusinessDay reports seven states with severe flooding and pest pressures in Kano, Taraba and Kaduna; these are national/regional signals, not a Plateau production estimate. [EVD-0023]
2. **Federal Government announced plans for three million bags of fertiliser and release of stored grains.** The Ministry says grain bought under the Bank of Agriculture's Guaranteed Minimum Price programme will be released, but the distribution modalities, exact grain volumes, dates, eligible beneficiaries and Plateau allocations were not published in the announcement reviewed. A separate federal information release says FEPSAN members reported about 29,000 tonnes of finished NPK stock and raw materials for approximately 148,000 tonnes of NPK 20-10-10; this is industry-reported capacity/stock, not proof that three million bags have reached farmers. [EVD-0024]
3. **Current Nigerian price platforms do not agree on comparable maize prices.** Useagrios shows a national average estimate of ₦74,277 per 50 kg and a Terminus Market, Jos estimate of ₦73,394 per 50 kg. ROL Global shows maize at ₦660,000 per tonne (₦33,000 per 50 kg equivalent) using a Commodity Nigeria fallback. These figures have different/unclear market bases and the Useagrios figures are mostly modelled estimates. Do not treat the difference as a real arbitrage opportunity. [EVD-0019]
4. **Global supply is broadly comfortable for wheat and coarse grains, but risks are rising elsewhere.** FAO's 19 November Food Outlook says rice, sugar and vegetable-oil balances are tighter; AMIS notes El Niño-related weather risk, phosphate supply tightness and Black Sea logistical uncertainty. Possible Nigerian transmission through imported inputs, freight, FX and substitute demand needs evidence; no Plateau-specific impact was quantified today. [FAO Food Outlook, AMIS November Monitor]
5. **Gmail review found no new substantive response from the seven contacts awaiting replies.** The Citizen to Government Platform email is the previously recorded automated acknowledgement, not a new substantive response. PLACOM remains a bounce. No new outreach should be sent merely because replies have not arrived.

### 2.4 Supply chain and distribution

The national harvest outlook does not guarantee lower retail prices. Transport cost, storage, quality losses, security, aggregation and market concentration determine whether additional harvest reaches consumers. BusinessDay's 22 November analysis specifically warns that logistics and storage bottlenecks may prevent output gains from translating into cheaper food. [EVD-0023]

A useful research question is whether Plateau producers can access storage, aggregation, grading and transport services at competitive terms—not whether more production alone is possible.

### 2.5 Processing and industrial intelligence

No new verified processor capacity, operating rate, buyer specification or industrial procurement notice was identified today. Existing investigations remain:
- Cassava starch: identify processors and industrial buyers; imports do not equal an accessible order.
- Cassava peel/HQCP: identify local peel volume, safe processing capability and feed buyers; do not assume wet peels can be fed directly.
- Potato: verify the existing public project assets and access before proposing any service.

### 2.6 Trade, finance and investment intelligence

- Federal Government's announced stored-grain release is intended to stabilise market supply, but the ministry has not yet published the precise quantities, dates or distribution/sale procedure in the source reviewed. [EVD-0024]
- The fertiliser initiative could support dry-season production if production, distribution and affordability translate into actual farmer access. Do not infer a Plateau allocation.
- A 22 November media report claims additional large-scale agricultural financing commitments. It was not corroborated against a primary government programme document during this review and is therefore excluded from actionable findings pending verification.

### 2.7 Global agricultural and agrifood intelligence

FAO's November Food Outlook reports generally comfortable wheat and coarse-grain supplies, with tighter balances emerging for rice, sugar and vegetable oils. AMIS reports that international wheat, maize and soybean prices declined in October while rice rose; it flags strengthening El Niño-related weather risk, tight phosphate supplies and Black Sea logistics uncertainty.

Potential transmission channels to Nigeria include rice and vegetable-oil import costs, phosphate/fertiliser availability, feed ingredient prices, freight and exchange-rate exposure. No measurable impact on Plateau prices was established today.

### 2.10 Creative and unconventional problem-solving

The most promising cross-sector hypothesis is a **commodity data-normalisation layer**: combine existing source feeds and trader quotations only after standardising commodity form, grade, unit, market level, timestamp and evidence level. It may help aggregators compare offers and reduce bad sourcing decisions. Before any build, test whether a specific buyer or trader needs this and whether current platforms or spreadsheets already meet the need.
