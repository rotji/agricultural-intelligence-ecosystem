## 2.1 Production and agricultural resources

### National production outlook

NAERLS' 2026 wet-season survey, presented on 18 November and published by ABU on 19 November, reports:
- Expected national cereal output of **29.39 million tonnes**, up 1.84%.
- Higher output projected for 13 of 17 assessed major crops.
- Cowpea: **4.46 million tonnes**; groundnut: **5.41 million tonnes**.
- Soybean: about **956,745 tonnes**, projected down 2.65%.
- Expected gains for sesame, cotton, plantain and banana.
- Seven states reported severe flooding; erratic rainfall, dry spells and crop pests remain risks.
- Severe fall armyworm reports in Kano and Taraba; Tuta absoluta in Kano and Kaduna; cassava mosaic in Anambra; livestock vaccination gaps and disease reports in some livestock-producing states.
- Fish production growth reported in Niger State (+114.3%) and increases in Gombe, Rivers and Kwara.

These findings are important for product discovery and risk monitoring. They are not completed production data, not a full-year census, and not a state-by-state marketable-surplus table. The fieldwork was conducted 31 August–6 September 2026. Plateau-specific LGA output was not established in the public article. [EVD-0018]
