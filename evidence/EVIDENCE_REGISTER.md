  1. Fruits and Veggies Global Ltd — Plot 10800, Du Road, Zawan, Jos.

## Evidence governance

1. Preserve source identity, dates and provenance.
2. Distinguish publication date from the date the market observation occurred.
3. Do not convert source-reported claims into independently verified facts.
4. Capture contradicting sources and explain differences.
5. Record when a source is inaccessible, stale, incomplete or unverified.
6. Keep personal, confidential or commercially sensitive information out of this public repository unless there is a lawful and necessary reason to include it.
7. Use stable EVD identifiers and never reuse them.
8. Keep evidence in its original context; a quotation without grade, unit, volume, location or delivery terms may not be comparable with another quotation.
